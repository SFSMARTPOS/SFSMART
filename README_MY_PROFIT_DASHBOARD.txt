SF SMART POS SYSTEM — MY PROFIT DASHBOARD

Added to the v19 base without removing existing features.

1. Temporary Accounts → My Profit Dashboard.
2. Token-wise report: Technician Commission, My Service Commission, My Item Profit, My Total Profit.
3. Monthly report: My Item Profit, My Service Commission, My Total Profit.
4. Technician monthly service commission: Service Profit, Technician Takes, My Service Commission.
5. From Month / To Month filters.
6. Excel export icon exports selected finished-repair history plus monthly and technician summary sheets.

Calculation:
Service Profit = Customer Charge − Item Sale Total.
Technician Commission = Service Profit × Commission %.
My Service Commission = Service Profit − Technician Commission.
My Item Profit = Item Sale Total − Item Cost Total.
My Total Profit = My Item Profit + My Service Commission.

Only Finished repair records are included in the My Profit Dashboard.
